The dining room
Our story

A lively, homely room that hasn't chased trends

Golden Dragon has spent years quietly doing one thing well: the traditional Kolkata Indo-Chinese food this city grew up on. Regulars walk in to a familiar, unpretentious room — full most nights, seated fast, and unmistakably warm.

The kitchen doesn't do fusion plating or novelty menus. It does wok hei, real garlic and chilli, and the kind of consistency that comes from cooking the same dishes thousands of times.
